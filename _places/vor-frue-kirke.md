## Sequence on the site

Before the stone church there was a **wooden church** (stave tradition) on this site; it burned during the Norwegian king **Harald Hardrada**’s attack on Aarhus in **1051**.{% include cite.html id="ref-aarhuswiki-vor-frue" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %} History 1 put the burn in **1050**, after which **Vor Frue** was built, and called it the oldest stone building in Scandinavia.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-30-steffen-history-1" %} Parish/lex wording is more precise: oldest vaulted stone church in the Nordic countries / oldest room in Aarhus (the crypt).{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}

The crypt (c. **1060**), built of **travertine** (*frådsten*) and fieldstone under Bishop **Christian** (who took part in the last raid on England), is the oldest vaulted stone church in the Nordic countries and the oldest room in Aarhus — a Trinity church with three aisles and three apses (each with an altar).{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-10-01-aritra-discovering-aarhus" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-louise-vikingebyvandring" %} Day notes recorded “Travertine” at this stop.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

Around it rose **Skt. Nicolai**, Aarhus’s **first cathedral** (stone church from the decades after 1060 / c. 1080 in parish accounts), outside the town ramparts — dedicated to **St Nicholas**, patron of sailors.{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}{% include cite.html id="ref-danmarkshistorien-vor-frue" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-10-01-aritra-discovering-aarhus" %} When the new **Skt. Clemens** cathedral was built inside the walls (from the 1190s), this church lost cathedral status.{% include cite.html id="ref-danmarkshistorien-vor-frue" %}{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}

## Blackfriars (Dominicans)

**Dominicans** (*sortebrødre* / Blackfriars / preaching friars) took over c. **1230–1240**, rebuilt in brick, and added the monastery wings. Their churches were open to the town.{% include cite.html id="ref-danmarkshistorien-vor-frue" %}{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %} After the Reformation the monks left (Aarhus Dominicans already **1530**); the church became the parish church **Vor Frue**, and the cloister a hospital for the sick and poor.{% include cite.html id="ref-danmarkshistorien-vor-frue" %}{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}

## Crypt rediscovery and crucifix

The crypt was closed off, used as storage/fuel space, forgotten, and **rediscovered in 1955** during restoration; rededicated as a church in **1957**.{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}{% include cite.html id="ref-danmarkshistorien-vor-frue" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-10-01-aritra-discovering-aarhus" %} Day notes said 1956/57 — close, but the find year is 1955.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

The Jesus figure in the crypt is **not the original**: it is a copy of the **Åby crucifix** (c. 1050–1100); the original is at Nationalmuseet. Shown as a strong, king-like / viking-hero Christ (looking **up**, **braided hair**, **muscular arms**, **big hands**; parish text also notes viking-period hair fashion and a Thor comparison).{% include cite.html id="ref-vor-frue-kirke" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-10-01-aritra-discovering-aarhus" %} The notebook also has **Kong Knud**; what that connection was is not remembered.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

## Runestones

Rune stones were reused as building stone in the church/monastery. Trap notes **four** among the Dominican foundation stones, including **Århussten 5**; Louise’s handout and History 1 say **six** Aarhus runestones (Århus-runestenene) at Vor Frue / the stop.{% include cite.html id="ref-trap-aarhus-dominikanerkloster" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-louise-vikingebyvandring" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-30-steffen-history-1" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}
