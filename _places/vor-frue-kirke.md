First church in Aarhus that is still standing; an earlier church was burned when Christianity was new.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

The **crypt** was used as storage, closed off, and rediscovered in **1956/57**. The Jesus figure there is not the original — described as a strong, viking-style Jesus linked with King Knud.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

They had **stave churches** here. **Blackfriars** (Dominican) connection; “Travestine” also noted in the day notes (uncertain reading).{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

Rune stones found in the church had often been taken down and reused as building stone.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}
