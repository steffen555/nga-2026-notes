## Town wall and stream

**Volden** follows the Aros town rampart toward **Graven**.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-louise-vikingebyvandring" %}{% include cite.html id="ref-stadsarkiv-volden-pustervig" %} Fortification from the **900s**: south along the stream, north at Graven, west at Volden.{% include cite.html id="ref-stadsarkiv-volden-pustervig" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

**Graven** — outside the rampart; water moat, probably fed from the stream when the defences were extended.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-louise-vikingebyvandring" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

The stream (**Åen**) gave Aarhus its name; water framed as gathering settlements rather than dividing them.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

## Borgporten

**Borgporten** at Store Torv: gate into Aros; **two towers** appear in Aarhus’s coat of arms — the gate itself is after the viking period.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-louise-vikingebyvandring" %}
