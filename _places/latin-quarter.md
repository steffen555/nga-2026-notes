## Town wall and stream

**Volden** names the former town rampart — you can walk the street that follows it. The fortification circled early Aros in the viking period (c. 900s): south along the stream, north toward **Graven**, west at Volden.{% include cite.html id="ref-stadsarkiv-volden-pustervig" %}{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

The stream (**Åen**) gave Aarhus its name; the tour framed water as gathering settlements rather than dividing them.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

**Graven** was the moat at the outer edge of town; street names still reflect the defences.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}{% include cite.html id="ref-stadsarkiv-volden-pustervig" %}
