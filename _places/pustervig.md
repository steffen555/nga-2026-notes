On the viking-theme walk the stop was noted as a former **trading place**, with an explicit “research” flag in the day notes.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

Published local history softens that label: today’s **Pustervig Torv** is modern (clearance **1915**; name official **1974**). The name more likely points to **smithing** (*kulpustere* / bellows) than to a named market. The southern stretch of Volden was itself called Pustervig until 1867. Archaeology here includes some of Aarhus’s oldest finds, among them **pit houses** from the **800s** — so the place matters for early settlement, even if “trading place” needs careful wording.{% include cite.html id="ref-stadsarkiv-volden-pustervig" %}
