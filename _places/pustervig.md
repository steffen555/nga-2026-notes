Louise’s walk lists **Pustervig Torv / Volden** as a **viking market place**; **Volden** runs along the Aros town rampart toward **Graven**.{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-louise-vikingebyvandring" %} Day notes also called it a trading place (flagged for research).{% include cite.html id="2026-09-26-steffen-cathedral-latin-quarter" %}

City archive: today’s square is modern (clearance **1915**; name official **1974**); the name may point to **smithing** (*kulpustere*). Southern Volden was called Pustervig until 1867. Archaeology includes 800s **pit houses**.{% include cite.html id="ref-stadsarkiv-volden-pustervig" %}
