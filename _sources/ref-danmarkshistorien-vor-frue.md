Excerpts filed 26 Sept 2026 from <https://danmarkshistorien.lex.dk/Vor_Frue_kirke_og_kloster_i_Aarhus,_1060-> (Ane Bysted).

- First named Vor Frue in **1558**; in the Middle Ages **Skt. Nikolai**; originally Aarhus’s cathedral. Mid-13th century taken over by **Dominican** brothers (*sortebrødre* / Blackfriars / preaching friars), who built the three cloister wings. After the Reformation (hospital from **1541**); today a foundation for the elderly.
- First stone church in the decades after **1060** as cathedral for the new Aarhus diocese; outside the ramparts north of the river. Travertine and fieldstone; crypt under the choir with three eastern apses. Dedication to St Nicolaus; a late source claims consecration in 1087.
- Crypt discovered by chance during restoration in **1955**; excavated by National Museum and rebuilt; consecrated as church again in **1957** (Skt. Nikolai).
- New cathedral inside the ramparts begun in the **1190s**; Dominicans founded a house in Aarhus c. **1230** and received the old cathedral. Dominicans: mendicant urban order; churches open to the public.
- Second half of 1200s: Dominicans rebuild (new choir, later brick nave; old north wall reused). South aisle mid-1400s; tower c. 1500.
- Dominicans left Aarhus already **1530**; Christian III made the monastery a general hospital in 1541.
