Excerpts filed 26 Sept 2026 from <https://aarhuswiki.dk/wiki/Vor_Frue_Kirke>.

- Crypt under Vor Frue is a remnant of Aarhus’s first cathedral, Skt. Nicolai.
- Still earlier there was a **wooden church** on the site, which burned during the Norwegian king **Harald Hardrada**’s attack on the town in **1051**.
- About a decade later Aarhus became a new bishopric and building of Skt. Nicolai began.
