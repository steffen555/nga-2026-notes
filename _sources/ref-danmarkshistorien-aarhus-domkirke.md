Excerpts filed 26 Sept 2026 from <https://danmarkshistorien.lex.dk/Aarhus_Domkirke,_ca._1200-> (Ane Bysted).

- Skt. Clemens is Denmark’s longest church (**93 m**); tower **92 m**.
- Predecessor cathedral outside the old town ramparts, where Vor Frue now stands; crypt of travertine still preserved there (Skt. Nicholaus).
- Present cathedral begun between **1180 and 1200**, probably under Bishop **Peder Vognsen** (1191–1202); on a site inside the ramparts that had held a smaller wooden church dedicated to St Clement.
- Romanesque cathedral finished shortly after **1300**.
- 15th-century Gothic rebuild: Bishop **Jens Iversen Lange** (1449–1482) — west tower with his arms, raised nave walls, star vaults, wider aisle windows, new bishop’s palace north of the church, altarpiece from **Bernt Notke’s** workshop in Lübeck. Finished under Bishop Niels Clausen (1490–1520) with enlarged choir and ambulatory.
- Frescoes mostly from the Gothic rebuild period (**1470–1530**).
- Reformation 1536: side altars (c. **40**) suppressed; new pulpit 1588.
- Restorations from 1865 onward aimed to recover a c. 1600 appearance; 1920s restoration gave the present look including the west-tower spire.
