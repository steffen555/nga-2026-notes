Excerpts filed 26 Sept 2026 from <https://trap.lex.dk/Aarhus_Dominikanerkloster>.

- Present Vor Frue incorporates remains of an older travertine and fieldstone church; crypt ruin excavated **1955** under the choir.
- Second half of the 1200s building phase: new brick choir; in the foundation of the east gable was placed a fine runestone from c. year 1000 — **Århussten 5**.
- In all, **four runestones** have been found among the monastery’s foundation stones over time.
