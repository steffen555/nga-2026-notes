Excerpts filed 26 Sept 2026 from Aarhus Stadsarkiv, “Ugens Aarhushistorie: Volden og Pustervig” (2024):
<https://stadsarkiv.aarhus.dk/nyheder/2024/ugens-aarhushistorie-volden-og-pustervig-grubehuse-og-voldanlaeg-blev-til-udeservering-og-brostensidyl>

- **Volden** names the former fortification around early Aros (900s, still viking-period Aarhus).
- **Pustervig** probably alludes to smithing (*kulpustere* / bellows), not a market name as such.
- Today’s **Pustervig Torv** is only about 110 years old; the area was built up until **1915**, when clearance for the Borgporten block created the square. Some of Aarhus’s oldest finds are from here, including **pit houses** (*grubehuse*) from the **800s**.
- Rampart: c. 3 m high circling the old centre — south along the stream, north at **Graven**, west at **Volden**; east along the coast poorly known archaeologically.
- Oldest stretch of Volden (south of Rosensgade) likely from the **1100s** as a stone street inside the rampart; that stretch was itself called Pustervig until **1867**.
- Northern Volden laid out after the ramparts were given up in the late **1400s**, on top of the former bank. Name “Volden” attested in writing from **1512**.
