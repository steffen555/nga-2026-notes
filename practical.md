<section class="section prose">
  <h2>The course</h2>
  <p>Every Wednesday from 16.30–20.00 and one Saturday per month{% include cite.html id="handout-welcome-2026" %}. Classroom lessons are usually at <strong>Gelinde, Balticagade</strong>, sometimes elsewhere — always check the program before each lesson.</p>
  <p>The program is updated regularly, along with invitations and extra events.</p>
  <p>You need at least <strong>80% attendance</strong> and a contribution to <strong>guide gold</strong> to go to the exams and obtain your diploma{% include cite.html id="handout-welcome-2026" %}.</p>
  <p>The target is to learn universal guide skills, do basic walking tours in Aarhus, and a few bus tours around Aarhus.</p>
  <p>Before guiding in season 2027, complete a <strong>first aid course</strong> (or provide an existing diploma). One will be arranged in May (own expense).</p>
</section>

<section class="section prose">
  <h2>Guide student card</h2>
  <p>You receive a personal guide student card for entrance to most Aarhus museums (and others around Denmark). When using it you are an ambassador for NGA — be kind and cooperative. Always bring the card on tours.</p>
</section>

<section class="section prose">
  <h2>Google Drive — Aarhus NGA 2026</h2>
  <p>For guide gold and materials. Teachers upload materials and exercises. After an exercise or assignment, upload your scripts after your presentation. In the end you will have a useful collection of “guide gold”.</p>
</section>

<section class="section prose">
  <h2>Practicalities</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>In the classroom you are offered coffee and water — bring a sandwich or snack for the long evenings. Baking for the class is welcome.</li>
    <li>On the bus: remember sunglasses. The guide seat can be warm and blinding.</li>
    <li>Dress for the weather and your comfort.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="section prose">
  <h2>Photos</h2>
  <p>Many photos will be taken in class, on the bus, in museums, etc. Say if you do not want to be in them.</p>
</section>

<section class="section prose">
  <h2>The group spirit</h2>
  <p>Many cultures, backgrounds and languages are in the group. Curiosity, politeness and generosity matter for guides — and in the classroom. The aim is a humorous, warm atmosphere where everybody can grow and contribute to the NGA spirit.</p>
</section>
